# Vector Foundry

Vector Foundry builds real-time observability infrastructure for high-traffic web applications. Our customers ship 4M events per second through our pipelines and rely on our analytics dashboards for incident response.

What we value:

- **Sharp technical writing.** Engineering decisions are documented, debated, and dated. RFCs are how we move forward.
- **End-to-end ownership.** Engineers own the cutover gate on their own migrations. We don't ship a migration we wouldn't be paged for.
- **Calm in production.** We hire engineers who have been through hard outages and are honest about what didn't go well.

We are a remote-first 90-person company, headquartered in Boulder, with engineering hubs in Toronto and Berlin.

# Northstar Robotics

Northstar Robotics builds remote-operation software for warehouse and field robotics. Our platform lets trained operators safely supervise fleets of robots from anywhere.

We care about:
- Reliable realtime control loops.
- Clear operator interfaces.
- Safety-focused engineering practices.
- Practical automation that helps people do higher-leverage work.

Engineering culture:
- Small cross-functional teams.
- Direct ownership from design through production operations.
- Bias toward measurable reliability improvements.
- Respectful technical debate and clear written decisions.
